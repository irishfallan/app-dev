# My Favorite Series: All of Us Are Dead
## Overview
*All of Us Are Dead* is a South Korean coming-of-age zombie apocalypse series released on Netflix in January 2022. It is based on the webtoon *Now at Our School* by Joo Dong-geun.
## Summary
> A zombie virus outbreak begins at Hyosan High School after a failed science experiment. Students are trapped inside with no food, water, or communication, and must fight to survive while the military tries to contain the epidemic.
## Why this is my favorite series
- **Strong character developmet**
- *Intense survival story*
- Unique mix of horror and drama
